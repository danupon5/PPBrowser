# Driving Safety

**Eyes on the road. The browser screen is for parked time and passengers.**

## While the car is moving

Android Auto covers PPBrowser with its "restricted while driving" screen, and won't open it again until you park. That is Android Auto's safety rule, and PPBrowser doesn't try to get around it.

## Listening while you drive

Audio can keep playing, like it would from any music app:

1. **Before you set off**, start what you want to hear while parked. It keeps playing when you pull away.
2. Use the **steering-wheel buttons**, the phone's media notification, or voice commands. Don't reach for the phone.

Video pages only play their sound while you drive.

## Before you drive

- In PPBrowser's Settings, check that **Keep audio playing in background** is on. It is on by default.
- On OnePlus and other ColorOS phones, set PPBrowser's battery usage to **Don't optimize**, or the phone may stop playback after a few minutes.
- Add the stations, playlists and podcasts you listen to as quick links or bookmarks, so you can pick them from the car screen later.

## If something goes wrong

If a page won't play or the sound stops, pull over somewhere safe before you try to fix it.

## The law

In many places it is illegal to hold a phone or watch video while driving. Know the rules where you drive. You are responsible for how you use PPBrowser in a vehicle.
